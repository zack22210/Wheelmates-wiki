## Understanding Technical Issues in WheelMates

WheelMates is currently in its active development phase, and players have identified several technical hurdles during the demo playtest. While many of these issues are common for early-access or playtest titles, the development team has already begun addressing specific graphical and gameplay bugs. This guide summarizes the most frequently reported technical findings to help you troubleshoot your experience.

## Reported Gameplay and Control Bugs

Community reports have highlighted a few recurring issues affecting navigation and input. If you encounter these, note that some have temporary workarounds:

*   **Main Menu Input:** Several players have reported that controllers do not function on the main menu. However, controls typically work as intended once gameplay begins.
*   **Camera Lock:** If a player dies during the double-lever pull cutscene, their camera may become stuck underneath the vehicle. This can be resolved by respawning the character.
*   **Lever Activation:** Some players have noted that the interaction for the final lever requires extremely precise positioning to trigger the "LB" prompt. If you are struggling to grab the lever, adjust your character's angle and distance slightly.
*   **Hook Mechanics:** A known quirk allows a player to remain hooked after using the "return" function if the hook button is not released simultaneously. Ensure you release the input to avoid unintended tethering.
*   **Movement Exploits:** Players have discovered that driving in reverse can allow speeds up to 70 in zones with a 50 limit. Additionally, there are rare reports of players clipping through specific vents, suggesting potential collision boundary issues.

## Network and Multiplayer Connectivity

Playing WheelMates online in co-op mode has revealed a few limitations regarding lobby stability and connectivity:

*   **Steam Invite Issues:** Some users have reported difficulty sending invites directly through the Steam interface. If an invite fails, players have found success by hosting a new netplay game from the lobby and having the second player join the new session.
*   **Reconnection Limitations:** If a client player is disconnected or returns to the menu, they may be unable to rejoin the host's active game. In this scenario, the host often remains in the session alone, necessitating a restart of the netplay lobby.

## Graphical and Performance Fixes

Early testing identified a specific graphical error affecting smoke rendering. Users with AMD GPUs (specifically those using driver 25.12.1) reported that smoke from car vents and other sources appeared solid black and non-transparent. 

The development team has confirmed that a hotfix has been deployed to resolve this issue. If you are still experiencing black, opaque smoke, ensure your game client is updated to the latest version. If the issue persists, verify that your GPU drivers are up to date, as the developers specifically addressed this for AMD hardware.

## Progress Blocking Issues

A significant bug reported in the garage area involves the pump activation sequence. Some players have encountered a state where all three pumps are activated and the lever is pulled, yet the vent remains shut and the game continues to prompt for the third pump's activation.

Unfortunately, restarting the game does not always reset this specific event trigger. If you encounter this, try verifying your game files via the Steam client or checking for any pending patches, as these logic-based progression bugs are common targets for priority hotfixes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a fix for the black smoke issue on AMD cards?**
Yes, the developers released a hotfix specifically to address the non-transparent smoke rendering issue reported on AMD hardware. Ensure your game is updated to the latest version.

**What should I do if my controller doesn't work in the menus?**
This is a known bug where controller input may fail on the main menu but work fine in the game. Try using your mouse and keyboard to navigate the menu until you are in the active session.

**How do I fix the camera glitch during the lever pull?**
If your camera gets stuck under the vehicle after a cutscene, performing a respawn usually resets the camera to its proper position.
