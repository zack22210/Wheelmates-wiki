WheelMates is a dedicated two-player cooperative driving adventure developed by Firevolt. In this game, players control remote-controlled (RC) cars to explore Professor Bloom's cluttered house, solve environmental puzzles, and uncover the mystery of how they got there. Because the game is built entirely around cooperative play, a second player is required to progress. WheelMates fully supports local split-screen co-op, allowing two players to share the same screen on a single system, as well as online multiplayer.

## Setting Up Local Co-Op in WheelMates

Playing WheelMates in local co-op is straightforward. Since the game is designed as a split-screen experience, you can plug in two controllers to play together on the same PC or console. 

The game features full gamepad support, including official compatibility with DualSense and DualShock 4 controllers. A hotfix resolved issues regarding repeated D-pad inputs on these specific controllers, ensuring a smoother local gameplay experience.

To start a local session:
1. Connect two compatible controllers to your system.
2. Launch the game and navigate to the main menu.
3. Select the local play option to initiate split-screen mode.
4. Choose your customized RC cars and begin the campaign.

## How to Use the Online Friend's Pass

If you do not have a local couch co-op partner, WheelMates offers an online alternative using a free Friend's Pass. This feature allows one player who owns the full game to invite a friend to play the entire campaign with them for free.

To set up an online session using the Friend's Pass, follow these steps:

1. **Download the Game**: The host must download and launch the full version of WheelMates. The joining friend must download and launch the free "WheelMates Friend's Pass" version on Steam.
2. **Generate the Code**: The host navigates to the Lobby, selects **Play**, then **Play Online**, and clicks **Host** to copy the unique invite code.
3. **Join the Lobby**: The friend opens their game, selects **Play**, navigates to **Friend's Pass**, and enters the host's invite code.
4. **Launch**: Once both players are connected in the lobby, the host can start the game.

*Note: Both players must ensure they have installed the same version of the game. You can verify your version number in the lower-left corner of the main menu.*

## Core Co-Op Abilities and Tools

As you navigate through the kitchen, vents, shed, and backyard, both players will unlock identical tools to help solve puzzles and traverse the environment. These abilities include:

*   **Grappling Hook**: Allows players to latch onto handles, pull levers, open drawers, and swing across hazardous gaps.
*   **Magnet**: Gives the RC cars a form of telekinesis, allowing them to interact with objects and pick up items (such as oranges) to move them.
*   **Magnet Tires**: Enables the vehicles to stick to metallic paths, allowing players to drive up walls and across ceilings regardless of orientation.
*   **Paint Gun**: Shoots paintballs to hit distant targets or trigger specific buttons within a time limit.

## Puzzle Design and Mini-Games

The puzzle design in WheelMates heavily emphasizes simultaneous actions. Many obstacles require both players to coordinate basic movements to unlock the path forward.

Common puzzle mechanics include:
*   **Simultaneous Inputs**: Pressing two buttons or pulling two levers at the exact same time to open locked doors.
*   **Scissor Lifts**: One player driving onto a lift while the other presses a button to raise them to a higher platform.
*   **Divided Paths**: One player navigating a path behind glass or from above, using their tools (like the magnet or paint gun) to clear obstacles for the other player.

Additionally, players can discover and play competitive mini-games hidden within the levels, such as **Tag** and **Coin Rush** (also referred to as coin collecting). These mini-games offer a brief break from the main cooperative puzzles.

## Tips for Managing RC Car Physics

The driving mechanics in WheelMates mimic the erratic and playful behavior of real-world 1990s toy RC cars. Because the game is a 3D platformer controlled via vehicles, some players may find the physics slippery or difficult to control in tight spaces like air vents.

To make your co-op session smoother, keep these tips in mind:
*   **Control Your Speed**: Avoid holding down the accelerator fully in cramped areas. Gently pulling the trigger helps maintain steering control.
*   **Use the Reset Feature**: If your vehicle gets stuck on clutter or wedged in a corner, you can trigger a manual reset. This causes your car to explode and respawn safely nearby.
*   **Teleportation Recovery**: When falling from high ledges, the game often provides an on-screen prompt to teleport back up to your previous platform, saving you from repeating tedious climbs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I play WheelMates entirely solo?
No. WheelMates is strictly a two-player cooperative game. It cannot be played alone, meaning you must play either via local split-screen or online co-op.

### How much does WheelMates cost?
The base price of WheelMates is $19.99 / £15.99 / €19.99. Only one player needs to purchase the game if you plan to play online via the free Friend's Pass.

### What is the average play time of the campaign?
Depending on how thoroughly you search for collectibles like custom car parts and neural cores, the campaign takes between 4 and 7.5 hours to complete.

### Does WheelMates support cross-play?
The provided sources do not confirm cross-play functionality between different platforms. Ensure both you and your partner are playing on the same platform and have the same game version installed.
