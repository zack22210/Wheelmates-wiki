## Is WheelMates available on Xbox?

WheelMates was announced for **Xbox Series X|S**, but an exact Xbox release date and first-party Microsoft Store page had not been verified as of September 3, 2026. Firevolt released the Windows PC version through Steam on September 1.

The original announcement used a 2026 console window. That confirms the intended current-generation Xbox platform, not a precise day or an Xbox One version. Key comparison pages and database entries are not substitutes for a Microsoft Store listing.

## What is known and unknown

The WheelMates concept is a two-player RC-car adventure built around cooperative puzzles. The released PC version supports online co-op, local split-screen, and Friend's Pass. For Xbox, the following details still require first-party confirmation:

- Exact Series X|S release date
- Xbox price and regional availability
- Friend's Pass implementation
- Online cross-play with Steam or PlayStation
- Xbox Play Anywhere or PC Microsoft Store support
- Xbox One support
- Console performance modes and download size

Do not assume that owning WheelMates on Steam grants an Xbox license. No cross-store entitlement was verified.
