## How much does WheelMates cost?

WheelMates launched on Steam with a **$19.99 USD standard PC price**. Steam displays the final amount in the shopper's regional currency, so the exact figure can differ by country, taxes, and any active promotion. Check the live Steam product page before purchasing rather than relying on an old sale price.

Only one full copy is required for an online pair when the second player uses the free **WheelMates Friend's Pass**. That makes the effective cost different from a conventional two-copy co-op game, although the purchaser still owns the only full license.

## What the purchase includes

The Steam listing describes a two-player co-op driving adventure and shows online co-op plus shared or split-screen co-op support. It also lists 21 Steam achievements. WheelMates is not presented as a single-player campaign, so buyers should arrange a partner before purchasing.

The Friend's Pass is intended for the invited second player. The owner launches the full game and hosts the session; the partner installs the free pass and joins. The pass does not become a second owned copy and should not be treated as a standalone full game.
