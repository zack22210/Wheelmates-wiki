## What is the Paintgun in WheelMates?

The **Paintgun** is one of WheelMates' named car abilities. Steam has a dedicated achievement simply called **Paintgun**, awarded when a player obtains the ability. Firevolt's launch materials also group the paint gun with car upgrades that add new ways for the two players to work through puzzles.

The verified sources do not publish a precise room, checkpoint, or button sequence for acquiring it. For that reason, this guide does not provide a fabricated unlock location. The ability should be treated as a campaign milestone, alongside Grapple, Magnetic Grabber, Magnet Wheels, and Phase Shifter.

## How the ability fits progression

WheelMates is designed around two-player cooperation. Its upgrades are not isolated combat rewards; they expand how the pair can interact with oversized rooms, environmental mechanisms, and hidden routes. When the Paintgun becomes available, watch the tutorial prompt and test the ability on clearly marked environmental targets before assuming every colored object can be changed.

If one player cannot use a newly obtained ability, compare the version number shown by both players and reload the current checkpoint. A co-op session can become confusing when the host and joining Friend's Pass client are not on the same build.
