## Introduction to the WheelMates Experience

*WheelMates* is a 2-player co-op puzzle platformer that puts players in the driver's seat of RC cars. Set within the laboratory of Professor Bloom, the game tasks players with exploring the home environment, solving cooperative puzzles, and uncovering secrets. Whether you are playing the demo or the full release, the core gameplay focuses on synchronization and precise vehicle control to navigate complex obstacles.

The game is designed exclusively for two players. Because of the heavy reliance on teamwork, the experience is best enjoyed with a reliable partner. If you are just starting out, understanding the basic movement mechanics and how to interact with the environment is essential for overcoming the game's more challenging segments.

## Getting Started: Co-op and Friend's Pass

One of the most notable features of *WheelMates* is the Friend's Pass system. This allows players to invite a friend to join their session for free, even if only one person owns the full game. 

To host or join a session, follow these steps:
1. **Launch the Game:** Both the owner and the guest should have the game (or the appropriate demo version) installed.
2. **Access the Lobby:** Navigate to **Play > Play Online > Host**.
3. **Share the Code:** The host should copy the unique invite code generated in the lobby and share it with their partner.
4. **Join the Session:** The guest selects **Play > Friend's Pass > Enter the Code** to connect.

Always ensure both players are running the same version of the game. You can check your current version number in the bottom left corner of the main menu to avoid connectivity issues.

## Understanding Vehicle Controls

The physics of *WheelMates* are designed to mimic the erratic, fun feel of 90s-era radio-controlled cars. This can lead to a steep learning curve, as the vehicles are highly responsive and can be difficult to manage on steep slopes or narrow platforms.

*   **Driving Dynamics:** Steering can feel quite stiff. Players often report that the car may turn too sharply if not handled with care. 
*   **The Return Key:** If you fall off a ledge or become stuck, utilize the "Return" feature. This is an essential mechanic that saves time and prevents frustration during tricky platforming sections.
*   **The Handbrake:** While the handbrake is available, some players find that it does not always provide the expected drift-like turn. Adjusting to the specific weight and handling of your RC car is a key skill as you progress through the laboratory.

## Tips for Solving Laboratory Puzzles

Puzzles in *WheelMates* often require both players to be present or to perform actions simultaneously. As you explore Professor Bloom's house, keep these strategies in mind:

*   **Synchronized Movements:** Many obstacles require both players to hit switches at the same time or coordinate movements. Communication is your most valuable tool; always let your partner know when you are in position.
*   **Environmental Interaction:** Look for objects you can push, scan, or interact with using your vehicle. Some puzzles require you to move physical objects to create paths, while others require activating specific power nodes.
*   **Managing Power Cores:** In areas where you must manage batteries or power cores, remember that you may need to transport these items across the map. Be careful when jumping with these items, as losing them can force you to restart a segment.
*   **Scan Your Surroundings:** Use the in-game scanner to harvest data. Not only does this help with progression, but it also provides useful information about the environment that can make navigating harder tracks easier.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

If you encounter technical issues while playing, the developers recommend several quick fixes:
*   **Progression Bugs:** If you find yourself stuck in the Backyard or Shed, ensure your game is updated. Recent hotfixes have addressed many progression-blocking collision issues.
*   **Controller Input:** If you experience repeated or erratic D-pad inputs on DualSense or DualShock 4 controllers, ensure your controller firmware and game files are fully up to date.
*   **Performance:** If objects or mechanics (like rope swings) are not appearing, ensure that both players have installed the latest hotfix.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I play WheelMates by myself?**
No, *WheelMates* is built as a 2-player co-op experience. The puzzles are designed to require two RC cars working in tandem.

**Do both players need to buy the game?**
No. Thanks to the Friend's Pass, only one person needs to own the full game. The second player can join using the free version.

**What should I do if I get stuck in a level?**
Use the "Return" mechanic to reset your car’s position. If a game-breaking bug occurs, verify your game files on Steam and ensure you are running the most recent patch.
