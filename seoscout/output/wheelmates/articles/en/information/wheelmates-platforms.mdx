## PC version and co-op features

The Steam version supports online co-op and shared or split-screen co-op. WheelMates is designed for two players and does not present a solo campaign. For online play, one owner can invite a partner using the free Friend's Pass companion.

Steam lists Windows 10 or 11, DirectX 12, and 15 GB of available space. The store page is the authoritative place to check current PC requirements, price, supported languages, and feature labels.
