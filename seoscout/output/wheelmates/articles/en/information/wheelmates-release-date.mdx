WheelMates was officially released on Steam on September 1, 2026. This 2-player cooperative driving adventure allows players to take control of tiny RC cars to explore the home laboratory of the missing scientist, Professor Everett Bloom.

## Game Overview
In WheelMates, players navigate oversized household environments, solving puzzles and uncovering the mystery behind the professor's disappearance. The gameplay focuses on exploration and cooperation rather than combat. As players progress, they unlock new abilities—such as magnetic wheels for scaling walls and ceilings, a rope for crossing gaps, and a magnetic grabber—that allow them to reach previously inaccessible areas.

The game features a narrative journal that tracks story fragments, allowing players to piece together the history of the house at their own pace. Between exploration segments, players can participate in mini-games designed to test their driving skills.

## Cooperative Play and Friend's Pass
WheelMates is built specifically for two players, supporting both online co-op and local split-screen play. A notable feature of the title is the "Friend's Pass," which allows a friend to join the game for free without needing to purchase their own copy.

To invite a friend using the Friend's Pass, follow these steps:
1. The owner of the game launches WheelMates, while the guest downloads and launches the "WheelMates Friend’s Pass" version.
2. The host selects **PLAY → PLAY ONLINE → HOST** to generate and copy an invite code.
3. The guest selects **PLAY → FRIEND'S PASS → ENTER THE CODE** in their game menu to join the session.

## Pricing and Availability
As of its September 2026 launch, WheelMates is available on Steam. The base price for the game is $19.99 USD, with regional pricing adjustments applied for other currencies (e.g., €19.99, £15.99). The developers at The Firevolt Team have actively supported the game post-launch with hotfixes to address progression issues, collision bugs, and controller input refinements.
