Understanding the controls in WheelMates is essential for navigating Professor Bloom's oversized house as a tiny RC car. The game supports both keyboard and mouse configurations as well as standard gamepads, including PlayStation DualSense and DualShock 4 controllers. Players use these inputs to drive, perform aerial stunts, interact with environmental puzzles, and deploy special traversal tools like the grapple hook.

Whether you are playing locally on a shared screen or online using the Friend's Pass, having a firm grasp of your vehicle's handling and accessibility features will make your journey through the laboratory much smoother.

## Supported Input Devices

WheelMates offers flexible control options to accommodate different playstyles. When starting the game or troubleshooting issues, players can choose between two primary input methods:

*   **Keyboard and Mouse:** A standard setup for PC players looking to steer and interact using traditional computer inputs.
*   **Gamepads / Controllers:** Full controller support is integrated into the game. This includes popular options like the PlayStation DualSense and DualShock 4, as well as other standard PC-compatible gamepads.

The developers actively maintain controller compatibility. A recent hotfix specifically addressed and resolved an issue regarding repeated D-pad inputs on DualSense and DualShock 4 controllers, ensuring menu navigation and movement remain precise.

## Core Traversal and Driving Mechanics

Driving in WheelMates is designed to feel playful yet grounded. The RC cars feature a balance of "stickiness" to the surface and erratic, high-energy movement reminiscent of classic toy cars. 

### Airtime and Stunts
When launching off ramps or navigating the multi-layered rooms of the house, you can control your vehicle mid-air. Players can perform playful maneuvers, including:
*   **Flips:** Pitching the car forward or backward during jumps.
*   **Barrel Rolls:** Spinning the car horizontally to land cleanly on all four wheels.

### Interactive Controls
The puzzles throughout the house require physical interaction with the environment. You will frequently need to use your vehicle to:
*   **Press Buttons:** Driving over or bumping into buttons to activate lifts, open doors, or clear hazards like escaping steam.
*   **Pull Levers:** Interacting with levers, which often must be pulled simultaneously by both players to progress to the next room.

### Special Tools
As you advance through the campaign, you will unlock tools that expand your control options. The most notable is the **grapple hook**, which allows you to attach to designated grapple points and execute a rope swing to clear large gaps.

## Relocation and Accessibility Features

For players who struggle with precise platforming or find themselves stuck after a bad jump, WheelMates includes a dedicated relocation control. 

This built-in accessibility feature allows you to instantly relocate your RC car back to a higher, safe point if you fall. This eliminates the frustration of having to repeat long driving sequences or difficult climbs, making the game highly accessible for casual players, partners, and children.

## Troubleshooting Control and Physics Issues

If you experience issues with vehicle handling or input lag, keep the following developer recommendations in mind:

*   **Slope Behavior:** A recent game update improved vehicle behavior on steep slopes, preventing cars from sliding or losing traction unexpectedly.
*   **Version Matching:** For online co-op, ensure both the host and the player using the Friend's Pass have the exact same game version installed. You can verify your version number in the lower-left corner of the main menu.
*   **Bug Reporting:** If you encounter an input glitch, you can report it to the developers via Discord or Steam. Be sure to specify whether you are playing on keyboard and mouse or a gamepad.

## FAQ

### Can I play WheelMates with a controller?
Yes. WheelMates fully supports gamepads, including the PlayStation DualSense and DualShock 4. The developers have optimized controller support, including fixing previous issues with repeated D-pad inputs.

### What should I do if I fall off a platform?
You can use the game's built-in relocation feature. This accessibility option allows you to quickly teleport your vehicle back up to a higher safety point so you do not lose your platforming progress.

### How do I use the grapple hook?
Once you unlock the grapple hook tool during the campaign, you can use it to latch onto specific grapple points to perform a rope swing and cross wide gaps.
