## A practical completion order

Finish the campaign first and collect anything you encounter naturally. After the final area, use the level or checkpoint selection available in your build to revisit missed activities. Track Neuro-Cores, Neuro-Memory Cards, Custom parts, scannable objects, Neuro-nodes, and mini-games separately; each has its own achievement.

The **100% Mates** achievement is the final umbrella goal. Because its official description requires both full completion and all collectibles, completing only the story levels is not enough.

## FAQ

### How many achievements are in WheelMates?

Steam lists 21 achievements.

### Are there achievements for every gadget?

Steam lists achievements for Grapple, Magnetic Grabber, Magnet Wheels, Phase Shifter, and Paintgun.

### Does Friend's Pass disable achievements?

The official achievement page does not document a separate Friend's Pass restriction. Achievement ownership and unlock behavior should be checked on the account playing the full Steam version.
